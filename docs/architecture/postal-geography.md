# ZIP request locations and claimable public resources

## Public location model

New requests require a supported five-digit US ZIP, preserved as text including leading zeros. Version 2 AT records publish only `countryCode` and `postalCode` in the location object. Server validation canonicalizes any internal coordinates to the ZIP representative point; ingestion retains the ZIP. Legacy version 1 records remain readable, but records without a confirmed ZIP have no public discovery coordinates and do not appear in nearby map queries. Existing federated copies are not erased.

The posting form does not ask for GPS or a street address. Exact request addresses remain part of the existing private exchange. ZIP areas are not an anonymity guarantee: some ZIPs cover small areas.

The map shows states below zoom 7, counties at zoom 7–9, and ZIP areas at zoom 10 and above. Clicking a larger boundary advances to the next level. Requests in the same ZIP remain together at street zoom; selecting a ZIP filters the request list. No request offsets or fuzzy circles are used. URLs preserve ZIP identity without per-request coordinates.

Resources are separate, uncounted pins at eligible public street addresses. Visible pins scale from 5 to 18 pixels with zoom inside an interactive target that also scales with zoom (16–26 pixels). Resources sharing an exact address open a resource list at that location. The map requests one page of at most 100 resources per selected area and discloses when more matches exist. Visitors can use ZIP search or move the map and choose Search this area to narrow the geographic query. Directory browsing retains normal pagination. Directory distance filtering and sorting use eligible exact addresses before pagination. Nearby resource links automatically search from the request ZIP representative point; those distances are from the ZIP area.

## Geography provenance and reproduction

The supported catalog contains 33,791 Census 2020 ZCTAs. It is not a complete USPS ZIP validation service. Unsupported ZIPs receive an explicit validation error. ZCTAs approximate ZIP delivery areas; they are not political jurisdictions. State and county borders are Census boundaries.

Source archives:

- https://www2.census.gov/geo/tiger/GENZ2020/shp/cb_2020_us_zcta520_500k.zip
- https://www2.census.gov/geo/tiger/GENZ2020/shp/cb_2020_us_county_500k.zip
- https://www2.census.gov/geo/tiger/GENZ2020/shp/cb_2020_us_state_500k.zip

Documentation: https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/geography/guidance/geo-areas/zctas.html and https://www.census.gov/geographies/mapping-files/time-series/geo/cartographic-boundary.html.

`scripts/build-postal-geography.py` builds the lookup and geometry bundles using the pinned dependencies in `scripts/postal-geography-requirements.txt`. Run `python scripts/build-postal-geography.py /path/to/extracted-inputs`, with shapefiles in `zcta/`, `county/`, and `state/` subdirectories and source archives beside them; outputs are written into this checkout. Each ZIP receives one parent county from its public representative point (nearest county for coastal gaps), and that county supplies its state. Cross-boundary ZIPs are counted once and retain their full ZIP shape. No requester position influences parent assignment.

Artifacts live under `apps/web/public/geography/census2020`: one state file and 56 county plus 56 ZIP bundles, loaded by state as needed. The manifest records archive, artifact and lookup checksums. This is versioned JSON, not vector tiles. All 113 artifacts and the 33,791 ZIP lookup/parent assignments were checked for parity. Total geometry is about 71 MB; detailed nationwide polygons are never fetched as one response. The ZIP lookup currently contributes to the main JavaScript bundle (about 596 KB gzip total); further code splitting remains a performance opportunity.

## Public resource seed

The regional catalog targets the 19 counties in the Chicago–Naperville, IL–IN–WI combined statistical area, using the [Census July 2023 delineation](https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/metro-micro/geographies/reference-files/2023/delineation-files/list1_2023.xlsx). This broader regional scope includes the Chicago metro, Kenosha, Kankakee, LaPorte, and the Ottawa area. It is a starting directory, not an exhaustive inventory of every service.

`services/api/src/db/seed-data/chicago-metro-public-resources.json` contains public libraries from the City of Chicago, permanent health centers from HRSA, fixed food pantries from Feeding Illinois's public Vivery map, and We Care of Grundy County. Each resource references its own source, retrieval date, public address, contact, county and coordinate basis. HRSA imports exclude school settings, mobile/seasonal sites, restricted-location names and non-address geocodes. Food imports exclude mobile-only programs, school/residential/shelter locations, closure notices and coarsely rounded coordinates. Pantry county assignment uses the ZIP's representative county; it is not an eligibility/service boundary. We Care's point is a Census address-range geocode. Publisher address points are not independently surveyed entrances.

Source checksums describe the fetched JSON evidence, including the paginated food directory joined in page order. Review source records and current eligibility before replacing a snapshot. Do not manufacture hours, guaranteed access or real-time availability. The closed Galewood-Mont Clare library remains in the source snapshot but is excluded from import. All imported operational statuses are `unknown`.

Imported places begin unclaimed and unverified as organizations. Inclusion does not imply partnership or endorsement. Public source evidence supplies address eligibility without fabricating accounts, requests, organization memberships or steward approval. Source eligibility expires 90 days after retrieval; review and refresh evidence before expiry. Ordinary re-imports preserve claimed edits and do not silently extend source expiry.

`national-public-resources.json` adds an HRSA national catalog spanning all 50 states, D.C. and supported territories. It uses stable HRSA site identifiers and excludes locations already present in the regional catalog. `scripts/build-national-resources.py INPUT.json --retrieved-at YYYY-MM-DD` rebuilds it from a complete FeatureServer `features` snapshot; the source checksum covers canonical JSON with sorted keys and compact separators. Unsupported ZIPs, restricted settings, nonpermanent sites and non-address geocodes are excluded. Invalid source website values fall back to HRSA's public health-center locator.

For staged expansion, `--max-new-per-state=5` limits **new** insertions per state or territory. Preview first: rerunning an applied batch advances to the next eligible records, rather than replaying the same batch. Existing records and claimed edits are preserved. The checked-in national catalog is larger than the initial live batch. Bulk publication requires the bounded-map application release; older clients download all matching pages.

`npm run db:seed:resources` previews the import using `API_DATABASE_URL`; append `-- --apply` to import. The default `db:seed` is also resource-only. The explicitly named showcase scripts remain development fixtures and must not be run against the public database.

For an authorized fresh start, first stop all application writers, take a custom-format PostgreSQL backup, verify its SHA-256 and restore it into a disposable database. Run the resource command with `-- --reset` to inspect the exact table scope. Applying requires `--reset --apply --database=<exact-name> --backup-sha256=<verified-digest>`. The reset truncates application tables and obsolete indexer shadow/rollback records in one transaction before importing resources. It preserves migrations, indexer checkpoints/freshness, platform access roles, signup invitations and maintenance/operational audits. Keeping checkpoints prevents replaying the historical stream simply because the database was cleared. Other databases, PDS accounts, object storage and runtime secrets are outside this command's scope. It uses no cascading truncation; unexpected cross-scope foreign keys fail the transaction. Restore the backup with writers stopped if rollback is needed, then restart and verify public reads and absence of old entries.

## Reviewed claims

A signed-in organization owner or admin submits a claim with evidence of authorization. Evidence is visible to its applicant and platform reviewers. Pending claims grant no editing access. An independent platform reviewer must verify the organization's current verification and relationship to the place before approval; members of the claiming organization cannot approve it. Listing locks serialize competing claims. Review reasons and changes are audited.

An approved, currently verified organization can edit name, usual hours, access requirements, website and phone through Organizations → Resource claims. Street addresses remain subject to the existing location review process. Imported source snapshots remain intact through edits. Reviewers can deny pending claims and revoke approved claims. Account export includes the person's claims; deactivation removes private claim evidence and redacts their audit actor. Deleting an owning organization unclaims the public listing instead of deleting the public place.

The community catalog adds public Vivery food-network locations and HUD-approved in-person housing counselors from HUD’s current API. Food imports retain explicit service-program tags, exclude mobile/restricted/closed locations, and reject coarse points or coordinates outside the stated Census ZIP boundary (with 250 m tolerance for simplified boundaries). Rejected locations require address review before publication; their coordinates are never moved to ZIP centers. Housing locations require a matching Census address geocode. `scripts/build-community-resources.py` rebuilds the normalized snapshot from the downloaded source arrays and geocode directory. Source hashes use canonical sorted compact JSON. Neither a pantry listing nor a housing-counselor listing guarantees current supplies, appointments, shelter beds or rental funding.

`national-government-resources.json` adds SSA field offices and VA public benefits offices and fixed Vet Centers. `scripts/build-government-resources.py SSA.csv GEOCODES --retrieved-at YYYY-MM-DD --ssa-published-at YYYY-MM-DD --va VA.json` rebuilds it. SSA offices require a single Census address match with matching state and ZIP; all government pins must pass the ZIP-boundary check. The SSA file is the official field-office download (publisher date March 9, 2026, recorded separately from retrieval). VA imports use the public locator's paginated benefits and nonmobile Vet Center results, excluding overseas, military-restricted, campus-only, virtual, closed, and non-normal locations. VA service tags come from its published service list. Government-office text is searchable alongside SSI, SSA, SSDI and veteran benefit terms; eligibility and appointments must be confirmed with the agency. Government ownership is not automatically claimed or verified.

The government builder also accepts `--pha PHA.json --pha-published-at YYYY-MM-DD` for the HUD Public Housing Authorities FeatureServer snapshot. Keep its publisher update date separate from retrieval. Only HUD `LVL2KX=R` street-level rooftop matches are eligible; ZIP+4 and larger centroids, PO boxes, ZIP-boundary conflicts and duplicate provider desks are excluded. Stable IDs use HUD participant codes. Services follow the published Low-Rent, Section 8 or Combined program classification; listings do not imply an open waiting list or available unit. The agency phone and HUD public contact directory support checking appointments and eligibility.

`state-benefit-resources.json` adds state-level public program locations. Rebuild Illinois benefits, WIC and SNAP outreach with `scripts/build-state-benefits.py SNAPSHOT_DIR --retrieved-at YYYY-MM-DD`; its docstring identifies the three official IDHS locator URLs and HTML filenames. Every imported pin must have a matching visible office record, a numbered street address, published coordinates and a passing ZIP-boundary check. Temporary closures, remote offices and specialized long-term-care processing offices are excluded. Program types determine service tags, and WIC is named explicitly. Hours and eligibility must be checked with the provider; the archived source HTML retains detailed notices.
